# World tour — every place

The island is ~600 m across. You start in the **Town Plaza**; everything else is reachable on foot, by vehicle, by glider or by clicking the map.

![[p1-map.jpg]]
*The island map (M). Places stay under cloud until you discover them.*

## Town Plaza
The hub: town hall with a glass dome, fountain, shops, stalls, benches, islanders. Pip by the fountain gives you your first goals.
![[m5-plaza.jpg]]
![[m11-plaza.jpg]]
![[m12-night.jpg]]

## Resort Beach and the pier
Loungers and umbrellas, beach balls with real physics, jet skis, the pier with a gazebo and a fishing spot.
![[m5-beach.jpg]]
![[m14-pier-jungle.jpg]]

## Halo Falls, the creek and the Hidden Cave
The waterfall drops from Mirror Lake into a plunge pool; a creek runs from there past the plaza to the sea. **Behind the falls** there's a hidden cave: a campfire you can rest at (skips to morning), a sleeping bag, glowing crystals, dripping stalactites, and an old mine cart that rides through the mountain to Driftwood Harbor.
![[w2-1.jpg]]
![[v2-4.jpg]]

## Mirror Lake
Still water on the highland plateau; fishing spot.

## Old Ruins
Ancient arch and a gate puzzle.

## Lighthouse Cape, the Observatory, Halo Peak
The lighthouse and observatory have interiors. Halo Peak is where the halo hangs; breeze lifts carry you up.
![[m12-lighthouse-in.jpg]]

## Whisper Jungle, Tide Pools, Sunset Lookout
Smaller hidden areas added in v0.9.

## Driftwood Harbor (north-west shore)
A little fishing town: docks, boats, nets drying, Captain Finn who buys your fish, and the far end of the mine-cart line.
![[p2-0.jpg]]

## Cloudrest (the terrace above town)
Three houses, a spyglass that gives a tour of the island, pinwheels, and the **snow globe** (shake it for winter). The plaza arch, once awakened, teleports you up here.

## Coconut Cay (out to sea)
A tiny islet: a hut on stilts, palms, a castaway called Robin, and a buried chest.
![[v4-1.jpg]]

## Winter (Easter egg)
Shake the snow globe on Cloudrest: snow falls, the ground, grass and trees turn white, and your breath shows. Shake it again for summer.
