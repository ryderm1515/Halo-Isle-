# Demo cheat sheet

**Before:** open https://ryderm1515.github.io/Halo-Isle-/?v=2, let it load fully (~80 MB), then click Start once so the sound turns on.

**Demo route (about 2 min)**
1. Walk around the plaza, press **1–9** for emotes.
2. **M** → click the Sports Deck → **Travel here** → bowling.
3. **M** → Cloudrest → shake the snow globe.
4. Jump off Cloudrest, press **Space** in the air → glider.

**If something breaks**
- Stuck → press **Backspace** twice.
- Activity won't start → walk away and back, or press **E** again.
- Slow → "It's a full 3D world in a browser tab."

**Defense checklist (from the brief)**
- [ ] Say the build sentence (point 1)
- [ ] Run the build live
- [ ] Show one failure and its screenshot (point 6)
- [ ] The decision that answered it (point 6; there's no ADR written for it yet)
- [ ] Show the same test after the fix (point 6)
- [ ] Name what still fails (point 10)
- [ ] Explain the rejected idea and which decisions were mine (points 2 and 9)
- [ ] Show no passwords, keys or personal data

**Likely questions**
- *Did AI make it?* AI wrote the code. I wrote the brief and references, made the design calls, and playtested every version.
- *Where do the models come from?* Blender, generated by Python scripts. Textures are free CC0 ones from Poly Haven.
- *How long did it take?* Nov 10 to Nov 18, with an earlier version on Nov 6 inside Squishy RNG.
- *Why doesn't it match your PRD?* It started as Squishy RNG. Halo Isle grew out of it and became the part I cared about, and I still need to update the plan, PRD and ADRs to match.
- *What would you do next?* A story at the summit, and outside playtesters.
