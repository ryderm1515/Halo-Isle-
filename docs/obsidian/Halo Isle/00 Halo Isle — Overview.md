# Halo Isle

**Play it:** https://ryderm1515.github.io/Halo-Isle-/?v=2 (runs in the browser; keyboard and mouse)
**Source:** https://github.com/ryderm1515/Halo-Isle-

A third-person 3D island you explore at your own pace: a little blue jelly character on a resort island with a glowing halo over the mountain. It's inspired by the *feel* of Wuhu Island from Wii Sports Resort, pushed in a Frutiger Aero direction (turquoise water, glossy materials, golden-hour light, optimistic early-2000s look).

![[w2-0.png]]

## The idea in one breath
Go anywhere, stumble onto mini-games, find secrets, and slowly uncover why the halo went dim. Nothing forces a path.

## What's in it right now
- **Places:** Town Plaza, Resort Beach and pier, Halo Falls and a hidden cave behind them, Mirror Lake, Old Ruins, Lighthouse Cape, the Observatory, Halo Peak, Whisper Jungle, Tide Pools, Sunset Lookout, Driftwood Harbor, Cloudrest (a terrace above town), Coconut Cay (an islet)
- **Activities (17+):** ring races (Plaza Dash, Beach Sprint, Falls Run, Halo Glide), Bubble Pop, Keepy-Up, Slide Rush, Driving Range, Archery, Bowling, Hoops 3-point contest, Power Cruise (jet ski slalom), Cart Rally, Mine Cart Dash, fishing, and an arcade with three playable cabinets
- **Getting around:** golf carts, jet skis, a hang glider, breeze lifts, a mine-cart line through the mountain, a portal arch, and click-to-travel on the map
- **Story and secrets:** 5 halo shards, 30 Sun Stars, a glyph-block puzzle that wakes the plaza arch, a ruins gate puzzle, a buried chest, a snow globe that turns the island to winter
- **Character:** one hero, procedurally animated (no keyframed clips): breathing, blinking, squash and stretch, planted feet, leaf that lags behind, emotes (wave, celebrate, confused, sleep…)
