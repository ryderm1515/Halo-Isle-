# Activities and systems

Inspired by how Wii Sports Resort scatters things to do around Wuhu: you **stumble onto** them. Every activity gives a bronze, silver or gold medal and keeps your best score in the Journal (Tab).

## Sports
| Activity | Where | How it plays | What makes it fun |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bowling** | Sports Deck | A/D to line up, W/S to angle, hold Space for power and let go in the green; hold A or D as you let go to hook it. 5 frames. | Swing animation, pins that tumble into the pit, a pinsetter sweep, **STRIKE!** with confetti and a camera swing to the celebrating hero, frame marks (X, /, numbers) |
| **Hoops** | Sports Deck | 60-second **3-point contest**: 5 racks round the arc, 5 balls each, last ball of each rack is a gold money ball (2 pts). Hold Space, release in the green. | Fast; you can shoot before the last ball lands, net swish, "ON FIRE" after 3 in a row, flaming balls |
| **Archery** | East meadow | Aim with mouse/WASD, hold Space to draw, release to shoot. Hold too long and your arm shakes. 3 rounds × 3 arrows. | Round 2 has balloons to pop, round 3's target slides on a rail; **the camera rides the arrow** and slows down before it lands; arrows stick in the target; BULLSEYE moment |
| **Driving Range** | West meadow | Aim line, hold to fill power, hit the targets. | Big "Nice shot!" / "BULLSEYE!" moments |
| **Power Cruise** | The cove | Jet-ski slalom through buoy gates. Shift = boost. | Real swell to ride over |
| **Cart Rally** | Town and beach road | Golf-cart time trial through gates. Shift = boost. | |
| **Mine Cart Dash** | Hidden cave ⇄ Driftwood Harbor | Ride through the mountain; lean A/D to scoop crystals. | Rumble, sparks, a long tunnel with timber frames |

## Races and challenges
Plaza Dash, Beach Sprint, Falls Run (ring races with a 3-2-1 countdown), Halo Glide (glider rings), Bubble Pop, Keepy-Up, Slide Rush.

## Fishing
Cast at a spot (pier, Mirror Lake, the cape, Driftwood dock). Wait for the float to dip, press Space to strike, then **play the fish**: hold Space to reel while it rests, let go when it runs or the line snaps. 10 kinds, sizes and records logged in the Journal. Captain Finn and Sandy buy fish.

## The arcade (Halo Arcade, east of the fountain)
Two coins a game, pays out coins for good scores, keeps house records.
- **Jelly Hop** — endless runner, double jump
- **Bubble Breaker** — brick breaker
- **Star Catcher** — catch stars, dodge coconuts, 45 seconds

## Puzzles and secrets
- **Glyph blocks:** three blocks (fountain, falls, beach) carry back to matching hollows (○ △ □) at the plaza arch → the arch wakes and becomes a portal to Cloudrest, with a travel animation (drawn in, spun small, flash, set down)
- **Ruins gate puzzle** in the Old Ruins
- **Buried chest** on Coconut Cay
- **Diving for pearls**
- **Snow globe** on Cloudrest → winter
- 30 **Sun Stars** hidden everywhere; 5 **halo shards** are the story

## Getting around
Walk/run/sprint/slide · swim and dive · **hang glider** (dive to build speed, pull up to climb; updrafts off rising ground keep you up) · golf carts and jet skis (Shift = boost, quiet engine sound) · breeze lifts · the mine cart · the arch portal · **map click-to-travel** · Backspace twice = back to the plaza

## Progression
Coins (stalls, arcade, items), medals, Sun Stars, shards, discovered places, fish log. The **wardrobe is free**: tints, leaves and six hats (straw hat, sun cap, flower crown, tiny crown, shades, headphones).
