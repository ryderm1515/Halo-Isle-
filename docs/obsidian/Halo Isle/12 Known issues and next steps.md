# Known issues and next steps

## Be upfront if asked
- **First load is slow** — the whole game is one ~81 MB file.
- **Only tested on my Mac and in a software renderer.** Older laptops may stutter.
- **Some key-art details aren't built** — the cable car and the wind turbines.
- **The story is light** — 5 shards restore the halo with a short scene at the summit; there's room for more.
- **Medal thresholds are a first pass** — not tuned with other players.
- **Feet can slide slightly at a full sprint** — the trade-off for a natural step rate.

## Next steps
1. A proper ending at Halo Peak, and story moments between shards.
2. Playtest with classmates and tune difficulty.
3. Split the build so assets stream in and the first load is faster.
4. More key-art matching: cable car to the peak, turbines on Wind Ridge.
5. Controller support.
