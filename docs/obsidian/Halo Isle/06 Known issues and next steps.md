# Known issues and next steps

## Known issues (be upfront if asked)
- First load is slow (~79 MB single file).
- Performance has only been tested on my Mac and in a software renderer; low-end laptops may stutter.
- Some areas are less dense and detailed than the key art (cable car, wind turbines, glass dome aren't built).
- The halo-shard story ends at the summit with a short scene; there's room for more.
- Mini-game balance (medal thresholds) is a first pass.
